## Current increment

WBS-007.020 / SCN-020 will compare operating configurations at 10,000 tickets per month and identify a justified recommendation or state that none is justified.

### Limits

- Separate measured evaluation inputs from staffing, pricing, acceptance-rate, infrastructure, and maintenance assumptions.
- Compare deterministic, guarded-LLM, and hybrid configurations under low/base/high sensitivity cases.
- Keep the 10,000-ticket monthly volume explicit and do not present projected savings as measured results.
- Recommend a configuration only when the stated decision rule is satisfied.

### Verification plan

- BDD: SCN-020 report estimates monthly cost and benefit at 10,000 tickets and states a recommendation or no-go result.
- TDD: labor, review-overhead, API/infrastructure, net-benefit, and break-even formulas.
- Integration: measured evaluation results and explicit assumptions feed each configuration scenario.
- E2E: a report-generation command emits the one-page business case without relabeling estimates as measurements.
